Pip's voice: rays and the talking face

Listening: red rays around Pip, moving with your voice. The left one is today's length, the others use the "Ray reach" slider. Speaking ("Talking star"): the star itself is the character. It turns once and grows a little while the corner pieces fly out round its sides and circle it; eyes and a mouth appear on the star, and as it speaks its lower arm drops like a jaw and its side arms lift like cheeks. "Round face" is the first try, for comparison.

Without a microphone the voice is simulated (syllables with short pauses). "Drive from my microphone" moves the rays and the mouth with your own voice, so you can see how they follow real speech.
